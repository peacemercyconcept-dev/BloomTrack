The problem

Onboarding to Stacks shouldn't mean twelve browser tabs and a Discord full of jargon.

01

Documentation is scattered

Wallets, sBTC, BNS, and NFT standards each live in separate docs written for developers, not newcomers.

02

The technical entry bar is high

Terms like "Stacking," "SIP-009," or "decentralized pegging" are thrown around with no on-ramp for the people who need them explained.

03

There's no way to track real progress

Most tutorials end with a link, not a credential — so learners can't prove, even to themselves, what they've actually learned.
